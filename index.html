LiQ

Co-Founder

Liquid staking on Mantle

A liquid staking derivative protocol built to be the native staking and rewards layer for Mantle's MNT token. LiQ opens new staking opportunities, improves capital efficiency and gives MNT holders a smooth experience while their capital stays liquid.

Holders can aim for the most yield or go deeper into DeFi on Mantle, without giving up liquidity to do it.

Robin Harvest

Founder & Systems Architect

Yield optimization on Robinhood Chain

An automated yield optimizer in the vein of Yearn and Beefy. It auto-harvests and compounds rewards so holders earn without managing positions by hand.

Launch vaults for Index Finance

  • Core vault that compounds rewards back into the position.
  • Growth vault that keeps a share of rewards and sells the rest to grow the position.
  • INDEX-ETH LP vault that auto-harvests and auto-compounds LP rewards.

Smaller holders get access to rewards that otherwise require holding 10k INDEX.
